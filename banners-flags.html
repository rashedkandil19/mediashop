FAQ

Banner & flag questions

Does the roll-up include the stand?

Yes. A roll-up comes as a complete set: printed banner, retractable stand and carry bag. We also print replacement banners for your existing stand.

Are the flags suitable for outdoors?

Yes. Our outdoor flags use weather-resistant fabric and UV-stable ink. For windy locations we recommend the reinforced options.

What file should I send for printing?

A vector file (AI, PDF, EPS) is best, but a high-resolution image works too. If you only have a logo or an idea, our designers will prepare it.

Can you install large signs?

Yes. For shop-front signs and large banners we can arrange fitting. Tell us the location and size.
