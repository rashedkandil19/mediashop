Finishes

Make it feel premium

The right finish changes how your print looks and feels. Here are the most popular options.

Finish What it is Looks best on
Matte lamination Soft, smooth film with no shine. Business cards, brochures, a premium feel
Gloss lamination Shiny film that makes colors pop. Flyers, menus, product leaflets
Spot UV Glossy raised coating on selected areas. Logos and titles on cards and covers
Hot foil Metallic gold, silver or color applied with heat. Luxury cards, invitations, packaging
Embossing Raised or pressed design in the paper. Logos, certificates, folders
Die-cut Custom shapes cut from the paper. Stickers, folders, unique cards

FAQ

Printing questions

What is the minimum quantity?

It depends on the product. Many items, like business cards or stickers, can be ordered in small quantities. Bigger quantities lower the price per piece.

What file formats do you accept?

Print-ready PDF is best. We also accept AI, PSD, EPS and high-resolution images. We can fix or prepare your file if needed.

What paper should I choose?

For business cards we usually recommend a thick coated board. For flyers a lighter coated paper works well. We'll show you samples and advise.

Can you match my brand colors?

Yes. Send us your brand colors (Pantone or CMYK values) and we'll match them as closely as possible.
