FAQ

Stamp questions

What do I need to order a company stamp?

Your exact company name and the text you want, plus your logo if you have one. For official company seals, some authorities require specific wording, so bring a copy of your registration if possible.

How long does it take?

Simple stamps are usually ready quickly. Tell us your deadline and we'll confirm the time when you approve the proof.

Can you copy my old stamp?

Yes. Send a clear photo or bring the old impression and we'll recreate it.

Can I put my logo on a stamp?

Yes. Send the logo file or a photo and we'll redraw it so it prints cleanly at stamp size.

How do I refill the ink?

Self-inking stamps use replacement pads, and pre-inked stamps are re-inked with a few drops. We sell the ink and pads, and we're happy to show you.
